## §1 Definitions

**§1.1 Accident.** A sudden, unforeseen and involuntary event caused by external, visible and violent means, occurring while this Policy is in force.

**§1.2 Chronic Condition.** A disease, illness or injury which has one or more of the following characteristics: it continues indefinitely and has no known cure; it comes back or is likely to come back; it is permanent; it requires rehabilitation or special training; or it requires long-term monitoring, consultation, check-ups, examinations or tests. Diabetes mellitus, hypertension, asthma, epilepsy, ischaemic heart disease, chronic kidney disease and rheumatoid arthritis are Chronic Conditions for the purposes of this Policy.

**§1.3 Cosmetic Treatment.** Any surgery, procedure or treatment performed principally to change or improve appearance, or to restore or improve appearance that has been changed by ageing, and which is not required to restore function lost as the direct result of an Accident, a Congenital Anomaly requiring functional correction, or the surgical treatment of a Cancer.

**§1.4 Day Case.** Treatment in a Hospital or Day Surgery Centre where the Member occupies a bed but is admitted and discharged on the same calendar day and does not incur an overnight room-and-board charge.

**§1.5 Emergency.** A medical condition of sudden onset which, in the opinion of a Qualified Medical Practitioner, requires immediate treatment to prevent death, serious impairment of a bodily function, or serious dysfunction of a bodily organ or part, and where a delay of more than twenty-four (24) hours would materially worsen the outcome.

**§1.6 Medically Necessary.** Services or supplies which are: (a) consistent with the diagnosis and customary medical treatment for the condition; (b) in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice in the United Arab Emirates and with recognised clinical practice guidelines; (c) not primarily for the convenience of the Member, the treating Physician or the Provider; (d) provided at the most appropriate and cost-effective level of care that can be safely delivered; and (e) supported by contemporaneous clinical documentation in the medical record.

**§1.7 Member.** A person named in the Membership Schedule in respect of whom the Premium has been paid and whose cover is in force on the Date of Service.

**§1.8 Network.** The list of Providers with whom the Company has a direct contractual arrangement, as published by the Company and amended from time to time. The Network applicable to this Plan is the **Gold Network**.

**§1.9 Pre-Existing Condition.** Any medical condition, illness, injury, disability or related complication which existed, or for which signs or symptoms were present, or for which advice, medication, treatment or diagnostic testing was sought or received, at any time in the twenty-four (24) months preceding the Member's Inception Date, whether or not the condition was declared or diagnosed.

**§1.10 Reconstructive Surgery.** Surgery performed on abnormal structures of the body caused by Congenital Anomaly, developmental abnormality, trauma, infection, tumour or disease, and performed to restore or improve **bodily function**, or performed to correct a functional impairment that is objectively documented in the medical record.

**§1.11 Reasonable and Customary Charges.** Charges for treatment which do not exceed the DoH Mandatory Tariff for the same service in the same Emirate, or, where a direct contract exists, the contracted rate in the Provider's price list in force on the Date of Service.

**§1.12 Waiting Period.** A continuous period, commencing on the Member's Inception Date, during which no benefit is payable for the categories of treatment specified in §7.

## §2 Scope of Cover and Territorial Limits

**§2.1 Geographic scope of elective treatment.** Elective (non-Emergency) treatment is covered only when rendered inside the **United Arab Emirates** by a Provider licensed by the DoH, the DHA or the Ministry of Health and Prevention. Elective treatment obtained outside the United Arab Emirates is **not covered** under this Plan, irrespective of whether the treatment would have been covered had it been obtained inside the United Arab Emirates, and irrespective of any cost saving to the Company.

**§2.2 Emergency treatment worldwide.** Emergency treatment as defined in §1.5 is covered **worldwide excluding the United States of America and Canada**, up to the Emergency sub-limit stated in §4.2, on a reimbursement basis. The Member or the treating facility must notify the Company within forty-eight (48) hours of admission. Cover for treatment abroad ceases at the point the Member is medically fit to travel; continued treatment abroad after that point is treated as elective and is excluded under §2.1.

**§2.3 Elective treatment abroad requires prior written approval.** Where the Member wishes to obtain elective treatment outside the United Arab Emirates, prior written approval must be obtained from the Company's Medical Director before departure. Treatment obtained without such approval is not covered, and no retrospective approval will be granted.

**§2.4 Emergency Medical Evacuation.** Emergency medical evacuation and repatriation of mortal remains are covered up to AED 150,000 per Member per Policy Year, subject to prior authorisation by the Company's assistance provider except where the delay would be life-threatening.

**§2.5 Country of residence.** Cover applies only while the Member's country of residence is the United Arab Emirates. Cover lapses automatically where the Member relocates permanently outside the United Arab Emirates.

## §3 Network Tiers and Reimbursement Basis

**§3.1 Gold Network — direct billing.** Treatment at a Gold Network Provider is settled by direct billing between the Provider and the Company at the contracted rate. The Member pays only the applicable Member Share under §5.

**§3.2 Outside the Gold Network — restricted reimbursement.** Elective treatment obtained inside the United Arab Emirates at a Provider outside the Gold Network is reimbursed at **seventy per cent (70%) of the DoH Mandatory Tariff**, and the Member bears the balance. Direct billing is not available outside the Gold Network.

**§3.3 Emergency care outside the Network.** Emergency treatment as defined in §1.5 obtained at a non-Network Provider inside the United Arab Emirates is settled at **one hundred per cent (100%) of the DoH Mandatory Tariff** with no network penalty applied, provided the Emergency criteria are documented in the medical record. Once the Member is stabilised, continuing care must be transferred to a Gold Network Provider; care continued at the non-Network Provider after stabilisation reverts to the reimbursement basis in §3.2.

**§3.4 Room entitlement.** Inpatient accommodation entitlement is a **private room** with a standard en-suite. Where a Member elects accommodation above entitlement, the difference in room rate, and any consequential uplift in per-diem-linked ancillary charges, is borne by the Member and may not be billed to the Company.

**§3.5 Correct price list.** The Provider must apply the contracted price list version in force on the Date of Service and the network tier under which the Member is enrolled. Application of a superseded price list version, or a price list belonging to a different network tier, renders the affected lines subject to recovery.

## §4 Table of Benefits, Annual Aggregate and Sub-Limits

**§4.1 Annual aggregate limit.** The annual aggregate limit for all benefits combined is **AED 1,000,000 per Member per Policy Year**.

**§4.2 Schedule of sub-limits.** The following sub-limits apply per Member per Policy Year and are inclusive of, not additional to, the annual aggregate limit in §4.1.

| Benefit | Sub-limit (AED per Member per Policy Year) | Member Share |
|---|---|---|
| Inpatient and Day Case treatment | Subject to annual aggregate | 0% |
| Outpatient consultations | Subject to annual aggregate | 20% co-insurance, capped at AED 50 per consultation |
| Diagnostic imaging and laboratory | Subject to annual aggregate | 0% when ordered by a Network Physician |
| Prescribed medicines | 12,000 | 10% co-insurance |
| Physiotherapy | 6,000 (maximum 20 sessions) | 20% co-insurance |
| Dental treatment (see §4.3) | 3,500 | 20% co-insurance |
| Optical (see §4.4) | 1,500 | 20% co-insurance |
| Psychiatric and psychological treatment | 15,000 | 20% co-insurance |
| Maternity — antenatal, delivery and postnatal | 25,000 (normal) / 35,000 (medically indicated caesarean) | 10% co-insurance |
| Newborn cover — first 30 days | 20,000 | 0% |
| Alternative medicine (homeopathy, osteopathy, chiropractic, acupuncture) | 2,500 | 25% co-insurance |
| Emergency treatment outside the UAE (§2.2) | 150,000 | 20% co-insurance |
| Emergency medical evacuation and repatriation (§2.4) | 150,000 | 0% |
| Durable medical equipment and prostheses | 30,000 | 20% co-insurance |
| Preventive screening (§4.5) | 1,000 | 0% |

**§4.3 Dental benefit — scope.** The dental sub-limit covers examination, radiographs, scaling and polishing (one course per Policy Year), amalgam and composite restorations, root canal therapy, extractions and the surgical management of dental Emergencies. Crowns, bridges, inlays, onlays and dentures are covered **only where required following an Accident occurring during the period of cover**, and are otherwise excluded under §8.14. Orthodontic treatment, implants, veneers and tooth whitening are excluded in all circumstances.

**§4.4 Optical benefit — scope.** The optical sub-limit covers one refraction test and one pair of prescription spectacle lenses, or the equivalent value in contact lenses, per Policy Year. Frames are covered up to **AED 400** within the optical sub-limit. Sunglasses, non-prescription lenses, and refractive surgery of any kind (including LASIK, LASEK, SMILE and PRK) are excluded.

**§4.5 Preventive screening — scope.** The preventive screening benefit covers age- and gender-appropriate screening in line with DoH preventive care standards, comprising: annual blood pressure and BMI assessment; lipid profile and fasting glucose every three years from age 30; cervical cytology every three years from age 25 to 65; mammography every two years from age 40; and colorectal screening every ten years from age 45. A screening investigation performed outside the stated interval, or without an age-appropriate indication, is not payable under this benefit and must not be reclassified as a diagnostic investigation in the absence of documented symptoms or clinical findings.

**§4.6 Accumulators.** All sub-limits accumulate on a Policy Year basis and reset on the Policy renewal date. Unused balances do not carry forward. Where a claim would exceed a remaining sub-limit balance, the claim is payable only to the extent of the remaining balance, and the excess is the Member's liability. The Provider is responsible for verifying the remaining balance through the Company's eligibility platform before rendering non-Emergency treatment.

**§4.7 Continuity of accumulators on scheme transfer.** Where a Member transfers between schemes underwritten by the Company without a break in cover, accumulated utilisation transfers with the Member and does not reset until the next Policy renewal date.

## §5 Member Share: Co-Payment, Co-Insurance and Deductibles

**§5.1 Outpatient co-insurance.** The Member pays 20% co-insurance on each outpatient consultation, capped at AED 50 per consultation. No co-insurance applies to a follow-up consultation with the same Physician for the same condition within fourteen (14) days of the initial consultation.

**§5.2 Pharmacy co-insurance.** The Member pays 10% co-insurance on the cost of each prescribed medicine dispensed, with no per-item cap.

**§5.3 Emergency room deductible.** A deductible of **AED 100** applies per Emergency Room attendance. The deductible is waived where the attendance results in admission as an inpatient.

**§5.4 No waiver of Member Share.** The Member Share is a contractual obligation of the Member. A Provider that waives, discounts, absorbs or fails to collect the Member Share, whether by rebate, credit note, loyalty scheme, package pricing or any other mechanism, is in breach of its contract with the Company. Claims in respect of which the Member Share has been waived are not payable, and amounts already settled are recoverable.

**§5.5 Services exempt from Member Share.** No Member Share applies to: inpatient and day case treatment; newborn cover in the first 30 days; preventive screening within §4.5; emergency medical evacuation; and dialysis and oncology treatment under an approved treatment plan. A Member Share applied to an exempt service must be refunded to the Member.

## §6 Pre-Authorisation

**§6.1 Services always requiring pre-authorisation.** Prior written authorisation must be obtained from the Company before any of the following are rendered:

- (a) all inpatient admissions, whether elective or planned Day Case;
- (b) all surgical procedures performed under general or regional anaesthesia, irrespective of setting;
- (c) magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), computed tomography (CT), positron emission tomography (PET) and nuclear medicine studies;
- (d) endoscopic procedures, including gastroscopy, colonoscopy, cystoscopy and arthroscopy;
- (e) physiotherapy beyond the sixth (6th) session in a Policy Year;
- (f) all dental treatment with a billed value exceeding AED 1,000;
- (g) all durable medical equipment, prostheses and implants;
- (h) psychiatric admission and any course of psychological therapy exceeding six (6) sessions;
- (i) any single claim, or any group of claims relating to one episode of care, with a billed value exceeding **AED 5,000**;
- (j) all treatment outside the United Arab Emirates other than Emergency treatment under §2.2.

**§6.2 Emergency exemption and retrospective notification.** Pre-authorisation is not required before Emergency treatment. The Provider must notify the Company **within forty-eight (48) hours** of the commencement of Emergency treatment, or on the next working day where the 48-hour period ends on a Friday or a public holiday. A claim for Emergency treatment notified after that window may be reduced by up to 20% for late notification, but shall not be declined solely on that ground where the Emergency criteria in §1.5 are met and documented.

**§6.3 Validity of an authorisation.** An authorisation is valid for **thirty (30) days** from the date of issue and is specific to the authorised Provider, the authorised procedure codes and the authorised quantity. An authorisation is void where: the service rendered materially differs from the service authorised; the Date of Service falls outside the validity window; the treating Provider is not the authorised Provider; or the clinical information on which the authorisation was granted is found to be materially inaccurate.

**§6.4 Extension of an authorised episode.** Where the clinical course requires services beyond those authorised — additional inpatient days, additional sessions, or an intra-operative change of procedure — the Provider must seek an extension within twenty-four (24) hours of the change. Unextended services are the Provider's liability and may not be billed to the Member.

**§6.5 Authorisation is not a guarantee of payment.** An authorisation confirms benefit eligibility on the information presented. It does not waive the Company's right to decline or reduce a claim subsequently found to be not Medically Necessary, incorrectly coded, incorrectly priced, subject to an exclusion, or otherwise inconsistent with this Policy.

**§6.6 Prohibition on splitting.** A Provider must not divide a single episode of care across multiple claims, multiple Dates of Service, multiple Members or multiple invoices for the purpose of remaining below the AED 5,000 pre-authorisation threshold in §6.1(i), below a sub-limit in §4.2, or below any other adjudication threshold. Claims identified as having been split for that purpose are consolidated for adjudication and the pre-authorisation requirement applies to the consolidated value.

## §7 Waiting Periods

**§7.1 General waiting period.** No benefit other than for Emergency treatment is payable during the first **thirty (30) days** from the Member's Inception Date.

**§7.2 Specified conditions and procedures — six months.** A waiting period of **six (6) months** from the Inception Date applies to: hernia of all types; haemorrhoids and anal fistula; benign tumours and cysts requiring excision; cataract; tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy; nasal septum deviation and sinus surgery; gallstones and cholecystectomy; hysterectomy; varicose veins; and joint replacement of any kind.

**§7.3 Dental and optical — six months.** A waiting period of **six (6) months** from the Inception Date applies to all dental and optical benefits, other than treatment of a dental Emergency arising from an Accident occurring after the Inception Date.

**§7.4 Maternity — twelve months.** A waiting period of **twelve (12) months** from the Inception Date applies to all maternity benefits, including antenatal care, delivery and postnatal care. The waiting period does not apply to Emergency treatment of an ectopic pregnancy or of a life-threatening obstetric complication.

**§7.5 Pre-Existing and Chronic Conditions — six months.** Treatment of a Pre-Existing Condition or a Chronic Condition as defined in §1.9 and §1.2 is subject to a waiting period of **six (6) months** from the Inception Date. Thereafter, cover is provided on the same terms as any other condition, subject to the exclusions in §8.

**§7.6 Waiver on continuous transfer.** Waiting periods under §7.1 to §7.5 are waived where the Member transfers to this Plan from another UAE health insurance policy with no break in cover exceeding thirty (30) days, and where the equivalent waiting period has already been served under the previous policy. Evidence of the previous cover must be provided. Where the previous cover was of a lesser scope, the waiting period is waived only in respect of benefits present under the previous cover.

**§7.7 New corporate schemes.** Where a corporate scheme incepts with this Company, waiting periods under §7.2, §7.3 and §7.5 apply to each Member from the scheme Inception Date unless the scheme was transferred with continuous cover under §7.6. A Member who joins an existing scheme after its inception serves waiting periods from their own individual Inception Date.

## §8 Exclusions

The Company shall not be liable for any expense arising directly or indirectly from any of the following.

**§8.1 Not Medically Necessary.** Any treatment, service, investigation or supply that does not meet the definition of Medically Necessary in §1.6, or that is not supported by contemporaneous clinical documentation in the medical record.

**§8.2 Cosmetic treatment.** Cosmetic Treatment as defined in §1.3, including rhinoplasty, blepharoplasty, abdominoplasty, liposuction, breast augmentation or reduction performed for appearance, hair transplantation, chemical peels, botulinum toxin and dermal fillers. Reconstructive Surgery as defined in §1.10 is covered where the functional impairment being corrected is objectively documented — for example, a septoplasty is covered where nasal obstruction is documented by clinical examination or nasal endoscopy, but a rhinoplasty performed in the same episode for appearance is excluded, and where both are performed the cosmetic component must be separately identified and is not payable.

**§8.3 Obesity.** Weight-reduction treatment of any kind, including bariatric surgery of any type, appetite suppressants and weight-management programmes, save where bariatric surgery is pre-authorised under an approved protocol for a Member with a documented BMI of 40 or above, or 35 or above with a documented obesity-related comorbidity, and after a documented and supervised twelve-month trial of medical weight management.

**§8.4 Infertility.** Investigation and treatment of infertility and subfertility, including in-vitro fertilisation, intracytoplasmic sperm injection, intrauterine insemination, ovulation induction, gamete or embryo storage, surrogacy and reversal of sterilisation.

**§8.5 Elective abortion.** Termination of pregnancy other than where medically indicated to preserve the life of the mother and certified as such by two Qualified Medical Practitioners.

**§8.6 Self-inflicted and substance-related.** Treatment of self-inflicted injury, attempted suicide, or any condition arising from the use of alcohol, narcotics or other substances of abuse, other than the emergency stabilisation of an acute presentation.

**§8.7 Hazardous pursuits.** Injury sustained while participating in professional sport, motor racing, aviation other than as a fare-paying passenger, scuba diving below thirty (30) metres, mountaineering requiring ropes, parachuting, base jumping or any comparable hazardous pursuit.

**§8.8 War and nuclear risks.** Any condition arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, insurrection, military or usurped power, terrorism, or from ionising radiation or nuclear contamination.

**§8.9 Experimental and unproven.** Treatment, drugs, devices or procedures which are experimental, investigational, or not established as effective by peer-reviewed evidence and not approved by the UAE Ministry of Health and Prevention for the indication treated.

**§8.10 Alternative medicine beyond sub-limit.** Alternative medicine of any kind beyond the sub-limit in §4.2, and in all cases where provided by a practitioner not licensed for that modality by the relevant health authority.

**§8.11 Non-medical charges.** Telephone, television, internet, guest meals, companion beds other than for a Member under 12 years of age, administrative fees, medical report fees, missed-appointment charges, and the cost of completing insurance forms.

**§8.12 Congenital anomalies.** Investigation and treatment of a Congenital Anomaly other than (a) in a newborn Member within the first thirty (30) days of life under the newborn benefit, and (b) where the anomaly produces a functional impairment requiring correction and the treatment is pre-authorised.

**§8.13 Vaccinations.** Vaccinations and immunisations other than those on the mandatory childhood schedule published by the Ministry of Health and Prevention, and other than post-exposure prophylaxis following an Accident or documented exposure.

**§8.14 Prosthetic dentistry.** Crowns, bridges, inlays, onlays, dentures, implants, orthodontics, veneers and tooth whitening, save where required following an Accident occurring during the period of cover as provided in §4.3.

**§8.15 Vision correction surgery.** Refractive surgery of any kind, including LASIK, LASEK, SMILE, PRK and refractive lens exchange performed for the correction of refractive error.

**§8.16 Care not requiring a Physician.** Custodial care, domiciliary care, respite care, long-term nursing home care, and any admission arranged principally for social, domestic or convenience reasons rather than for active treatment.

**§8.17 Services already funded.** Any charge recoverable from another insurer, from a third party liable at law, from an employer under workers' compensation arrangements, or from a government scheme.

**§8.18 Services not rendered.** Any charge for a service, item or attendance that was not actually delivered to the Member, or that is not evidenced in the clinical record.

## §9 Claims Procedure and Timelines

**§9.1 Direct billing submission.** A Network Provider must submit a claim through the mandated data exchange within **thirty (30) days** of the Date of Service, or of the discharge date for an inpatient episode. Claims submitted after that window may be declined for late submission and may not be billed to the Member.

**§9.2 Reimbursement claims.** A Member seeking reimbursement must submit the claim, with original itemised invoices, receipts, the prescription and the clinical report, within **sixty (60) days** of the Date of Service.

**§9.3 Adjudication window.** The Company shall adjudicate and remit, or issue a denial with a specific denial code and reason, within **thirty (30) days** of receipt of a complete claim.

**§9.4 Requests for information.** Where the Company requests additional clinical information, the adjudication clock is suspended from the date of the request until the information is received. The Provider must respond within fifteen (15) days. A claim unanswered after two requests may be closed as unsubstantiated.

**§9.5 Resubmission.** A denied claim may be resubmitted once, with corrective information, within **thirty (30) days** of the remittance advice. A resubmission must reference the original claim identifier and must state what has changed.

**§9.6 Audit hold.** Where a claim is selected for clinical or financial audit, the adjudication clock is suspended for the duration of the audit, subject to a maximum of sixty (60) days. The Provider shall be notified that an audit hold is in force.

**§9.7 Provider records and audit access.** The Provider shall retain the complete clinical and financial record for each claim for a minimum of **ten (10) years** and shall make it available to the Company's auditors on reasonable notice. Failure to produce the record on request creates a rebuttable presumption that the service was not rendered as billed.

**§9.8 Recovery.** Amounts paid in respect of claims subsequently found to be incorrectly coded, incorrectly priced, not Medically Necessary, subject to an exclusion, or not rendered, are recoverable by the Company by set-off against future remittances or by direct demand.

## §10 Coordination, Fraud and Termination

**§10.1 Coordination of benefits.** Where a Member is covered under more than one policy, this Policy pays on a coordinated basis and the total recovery shall not exceed the actual incurred expense.

**§10.2 Fraud.** Where a claim is in any respect fraudulent, or where fraudulent means are used by the Member, the Provider or anyone acting on their behalf to obtain a benefit, the Company may decline the claim in full, recover any amounts paid, terminate cover in respect of the Member, terminate the Provider agreement, and report the matter to the relevant regulator and law enforcement authority.

**§10.3 Misrepresentation.** Non-disclosure or misrepresentation of a material fact at inception or renewal entitles the Company to avoid the Policy from inception or to vary the terms retrospectively.

**§10.4 Termination of cover.** Cover in respect of a Member terminates on the earliest of: the Policy expiry date; the date the Member ceases to be eligible under the scheme; the date of the Member's death; the date the Member permanently relocates outside the United Arab Emirates; or the date of termination for fraud under §10.2. No benefit is payable for services rendered after the termination date, irrespective of when the underlying condition arose.

**§10.5 Governing law.** This Policy is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates as applied in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi, and by the regulations of the Department of Health – Abu Dhabi and the Central Bank of the UAE in its insurance supervision capacity.
